📷Recording a play from an image

Cappers post personal betting-slip images in Capper_plays (1513186440278704178). Open the bot's review controls, check selections, odds and units, and use Edit bet or Enter units if needed. Press Confirm and record only after checking the extracted data. These plays credit the individual capper and feed that capper's website homepage, never the official team tracker. Their cards use the same generated-image layout as official picks: the original uncropped slip above, bet details below-left, and matching cached team/player artwork or the server icon below-right. The embed retains the capper's name and avatar, the image is labeled CAPPER TRACKING, and personal plays still do not create BANGs. Settlements and edits regenerate the image from privately stored originals; text-only plays show a no-slip panel. Existing posts are not bulk-reposted.

Confirmation creates one tracked card in Capper_plays, normally replacing the upload. React on that card, not the deleted upload or review prompt. If reposting fails, a new card is published without deleting the upload; the new card is tracked. There is no extra official or confirmation-channel copy.

✍️Recording a play manually

Use /play in Capper_plays when you have no image. You need the capper role and any configured official-role permissions. Follow the input form for units, selections and American odds; multi-leg plays collect the remaining legs and calculate combined odds. The resulting personal card stays in Capper_plays. Official plays cannot bypass ballot approval through this command.

⭐Team ballots and official picks

Post a native single-choice Discord poll in Cappers_Ballet (1559647368650166322), with three choices beginning with Yes, No, and Pass. Attach the bet image or post it immediately afterward from the same account. Without an image, include the complete selection and American odds in the poll text or your immediately following message. Missing or ambiguous selections/odds require staff review; the bot never invents them.

Only finalized polls are evaluated, once per minute. Approval requires at least three Yes votes and strictly more Yes than No. Pass is ignored for approval: 3 Yes = 3 stars / 1u; 4 Yes = 4 stars / 2u; 5 Yes = 5 stars / 3u. Two or fewer Yes votes, ties and No-majorities are not posted. More than five Yes votes or malformed polls require staff review.

Approved picks are posted in Official_Playmaker_Plays (1560077460488458392) using only the bot's own name and avatar, never a capper's identity, and mention the HIGHROLLER role. The bot generates one fixed-width image inside the embed: the original uncropped slip spans the top, bet details occupy the lower-left panel, and a matching team logo or player portrait occupies the lower-right panel. Artwork is matched from cached API data only; ambiguous selections, parlays, and unavailable player images use the server icon. The server must have an icon configured. Text-only ballots receive a clearly labeled no-slip panel. Image descriptions retain the selection text. Official plays count toward the automatic team unit summary, not individual capper records.

When an Official Playmaker Picks play is settled as a win by reaction, /settle, or a confirmed settlement suggestion, the bot automatically posts a BANG in Winning Slips (1328168927812911165) and tags HIGHROLLER. It uses the same generated-card layout: original slip above, stake/odds/net profit in units and the team's gold-star rating below-left, artwork below-right, and a link to the original pick. Capper-tracked plays do not create BANGs.

Reopening or correcting a play updates its existing BANG with a prominent correction instead of deleting it. The image remains inside the embed; corrections and subsequent wins never send another role ping. A one-minute durable event scan catches saved result changes, including maintenance-script settlements or interrupted bot updates; it does not replay historical wins on deployment. Pending live events wait while testing mode is enabled. Test-mode corrections and messages stay isolated from live channels.

Staff recovery tools are private and restricted to server managers or configured operators in the configured server. Use /bang_status play_id: to inspect claimed, delivered, failed, or uncertain delivery. Inspect Winning Slips before recovery: /bang_recover play_id: message_id: links an existing bot-owned BANG without resending or pinging; /bang_retry play_id: confirmed_no_message:true is only for cases where staff has checked that no BANG exists. Active claims require a ten-minute wait. Failed or uncertain sends are never automatically resent. A missing/deleted tracked message requires staff review, not automatic replacement.

BANG deployment: stop the old bot, apply 20261017000000_bang_delivery_recovery.sql after the existing BANG and capper/ballot migrations, then deploy and restart the updated bot so its staff commands sync. The migration adds private original-slip/artwork storage for regeneration and a durable result-event queue, and replaces the old BANG RPCs. Existing delivered messages and uncertain claims are retained. Old posts are not bulk-redrawn: available originals are captured when a card is next refreshed or settled. Startup checks validate the Official Picks and Winning Slips channels, HIGHROLLER role and mentionability, required posting/image/history permissions, and migration availability; failures alert staff. WINNING_SLIPS_CHANNEL_ID selects the destination; the legacy DISCORD_PLATINUM_ROLE_ID setting selects HIGHROLLER.

All posts in Cappers_Ballet are deleted daily at 3:00 AM Eastern. Save any ballot details you need before cleanup; open or interrupted ballots are not carried over for later processing.

Deployment requires the separate capper/ballot database migration and a bot restart. The first scan includes existing channel ballots; later scans resume a saved cursor. Durable claims prevent duplicate publication. Failed or interrupted publications alert staff and are not automatically retried: reconcile the saved play and any Discord post before changing the ballot state. Existing records remain classified as official; do not infer historical personal credit without reviewing original sources. Old automatic Top Playmakers messages are not updated or deleted; staff may remove them manually.

For a late image or correction, trusted staff can set capper_ballots.source_message_id to its message ID in the same ballot channel. The displayed tracking era starts October 14, 2026, at midnight Eastern. 📈Website Data Models: six competitions

Open /models with verified OPERATOR/Owner access. Choose NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, NCAA Football or NCAA Men's Basketball, then choose an upcoming matchup. The NFL Lab link appears only under the NFL tab.

The page shows stored projections from the Playmaker ratings model (ratings-v2), refreshed by the bot every 30 minutes. A game stays listed until one hour after its scheduled start.

Ratings: every team carries offensive and defensive ratings that update after each game, adjusted for opponent strength and a learned home advantage, and partially regressed between seasons. At least three prior games per team and 30 league games are required; otherwise no projection is shown.

Learned adjustments: rest days and back-to-backs, travel distance between venues, and outdoor weather (temperature, wind and precipitation from Open-Meteo) for NFL, MLB, NHL and NBA venues. NCAA has no venue data, so it has no travel or weather adjustment. NFL play-by-play efficiency (EPA) is used only when it improves the backtest; it currently does not.

NFL injuries: players listed Out or Doubtful reduce their team's projection using fixed position weights (QB weighted most). This is a heuristic, not a depth-chart model.

Probabilities: hockey and baseball use a Poisson score model; football and basketball use a calibrated normal margin.

Market comparison: the consensus sportsbook moneyline (no-vig), spread and total are shown beside the model with the model edge, cover and over probabilities. Market prices are never inputs to the projection.

Track record: a walk-forward backtest projects each past game using only information available before it, tunes on the earlier half and reports the later half against the old score-only baseline (Brier score, log loss, accuracy, margin and total error, plus calibration buckets).

Unresolved provider teams and NFL preseason games are excluded. Duplicate fixtures are merged across provider IDs/seasons by teams and exact start time so selections and history count the matchup once. Scored finals take precedence over duplicate schedule rows; fresher data is preferred.

These are experimental estimates, not betting recommendations. Injury, weather and odds feeds can be incomplete; historical weather only covers the last 92 days; odds cannot be backtested. Read sample sizes, update times and the track record.

Loading or reloading the cache makes no provider request. Non-football odds reserve five requests upfront for a bounded league/season scan; unused reservations are not refunded. Other refreshes reserve one. The bot must run the research worker. Pending requests expire after 15 minutes. Interrupted in-flight requests are marked failed after 10 minutes rather than replayed automatically; inspect cache timestamps before requesting again. Status/error messages appear in Refresh activity, which polls the cache while your account has active requests.

🔑Commands for operators and server managers

CommandWhat it does
/tracker_start date_value:YYYY-MM-DDCounts only plays settled on or after this date, for example 2026-09-28. Everything earlier is hidden from the tracker but is never deleted.
/tracker_start date_value:clearCounts every play again.

Both settings revert to their .env defaults when the bot restarts. Set TRACKER_START_DATE or TESTING in .env to make a change permanent.

📊How units are scored

Wins pay the true price of the bet. Risking 2 units at +250 and winning returns +5 units; the same 2 units at −110 returns +1.82 units. A loss always costs the full amount risked. Voids and regrades count as zero. Your record displays as wins-losses, and the net unit figure reflects actual profit rather than a flat win count.

🚨If something does not appear

Check the bot, role, channel, account identity and source update time. If a play will not settle by reaction, use the authorized /settle picker. Expired controls require rerunning the command. For website errors, deploy the latest frontend and reload; use Retry when offered.

Sports-data failure alerts name a job; check its traceback before retrying. A shared-allowance denial is not fixed by restarting. A model can legitimately have insufficient history or no matching game. Unresolved-team and duplicate counts are data-quality notices, not fabricated estimates. Provider or database errors must not be treated as empty successful results.
